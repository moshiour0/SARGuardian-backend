## Fixed reconstruction policy

The script uses one numpy.default_rng(20260907) generator across chronological pairs; samples up to 1,200 unique candidate centres from finite displacement-magnitude pixels; forms a pixel-index disk with nominal area equal to the crown displacement-valid count; rejects regions with less than 60% valid support; and computes the strict empirical percentile as 100 times the fraction of accepted null medians below the observed crown median. Crown selection uses the deposited endpoint/searchsorted convention. The exact archive SHA, raster member identity, pair ordering and count fields are checked.

These settings are a source-inspired reconstruction, not a recovered copy of the missing original displacement-null script. The paper describes compact, size-matched regions placed on valid ground and the released sibling code/polygon_null.py gives a seeded random-disk/60%-support policy for directional coherence; the specific displacement-null generator and original draw/rejection history are absent. The accepted counts below are generated by this reconstruction, not claimed to be author counts.
